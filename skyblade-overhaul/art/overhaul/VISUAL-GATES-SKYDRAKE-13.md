The rebuilt candidate has a more independent arm silhouette but its torso join forms a fan-shaped crease and angular socket. This is visible in `after-walk.png`, `after-attack.png` and `lod-attack.png`; the clay attack captures confirm a geometry issue rather than only a color-transfer artifact.

Actual game captures include ordinary ground-tail and flight attack cycles, using existing enemy, player and lock-on camera updates in isolated profiles. High swoop framing remains a known limitation. Ground frame 052 and late flight frame 448 are useful comparison views, not whole-cycle visual approval.
