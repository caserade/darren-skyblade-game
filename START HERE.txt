DARREN: SKYBLADE HUNT - PC EDITION

PLAY (WINDOWS APP)
The folder "Darren Skyblade App" next to this one holds "Darren Skyblade.exe": double-click it.
Nothing else to install. It checks GitHub for updates (Claude's channel and the ChatGPT channel)
when it starts and every hour; see Hangar > Update. Game > Import a ChatGPT zip installs a zip
from ChatGPT. Windows may warn the first time (unsigned app): More info > Run anyway.

PLAY (BROWSER)
Double-click "Play Darren.cmd". Your browser opens the game at http://127.0.0.1:4319/
(it needs Node.js LTS from nodejs.org; it is already on this PC).
Double-click "Stop Darren.cmd" when you are finished.
No Node.js? Open dist\DarrenSkyblade.html instead (one file, works offline; its save is separate).

WHAT IS IN IT
Story: Chapter 1 The Trail to Cloudroost Peak, Chapter 2 Firewood for Mom, Chapter 3 Stormglass Reach.
Adventure: Cloud Utopia with Dumpling, the Sky Drake (Gale Spires), the Heavyweight (Sparkstone Mesa)
and Chomp Rex (Thorn Wastes). Field guide: Hangar > Field guide.
WASD move, mouse camera, J slash, K heavy, Space jump, V fly, Shift dash, Q lock on, E use, P Hangar.
Controllers work in the menus too. Hangar > Settings > Graphics: Automatic, High detail or Performance.

SAVES
The game saves in the browser you play in, at http://127.0.0.1:4319/ . The earlier "Merged PC" copy used the
same address, so its progress carries over. The claude.ai version keeps its own separate save.
