MODELING QUALITY
Model continuous sculpted forms with intentional silhouette and facial planes.
Avoid recreating the earlier sphere-and-box placeholder assembly. Keep coherent

around joints and mouth. The sheets are AI-generated visual concepts, not
dimensionally exact CAD drawings: resolve small view inconsistencies into one
consistent model while preserving the approved appearance. Show full-body

MATERIALS AND SIZE
Keep the colorful cartoon look, matte surfaces, subtle scales and soft painted
gradients. Avoid glossy plastic skin and heavy photorealistic detail. The game
is browser-based, so prioritize efficient meshes, shared textures and modest
download size. Suggested starting budgets, adjustable after visual testing:
20,000–35,000 triangles per main model; a 1K color atlas and 1K normal map where
needed, with a packed roughness/metalness map only when it adds useful detail.
Supply a lighter LOD and 512-pixel texture option. Keep editable high-quality
source assets separate from compact runtime exports. Report actual sizes.

RIG
Use a consistent root at ground level, hips, spine, head, jaw, limb chains,
feet and toe controls, and multiple tail bones. A needs several neck bones
and wing-arm/finger chains; B needs compact wing chains and restrained belly
secondary motion. C needs a controllable jaw, short forearms and clear foot
placement. Keep eyes and eyebrows expressive with bones or morph targets.
Provide clean weights, neutral rest pose and a labeled control rig. Use
in-place animations for game-controlled movement; export glTF with +Y up
and document the model's forward direction and scale. Check wing, belly,
tail and mouth intersections throughout the animation range.
